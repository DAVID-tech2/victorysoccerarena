Events

Bring your people.

Corporate football,School sports competitions, tournaments, leagues and private sports events — all at Victory.

Your event, our arena.

Victory Soccer Arena is built to host. From company football days to school sports competitions to competitive tournaments, from weekly leagues to private group bookings — the pitch, the facilities and the atmosphere are here to make your event work.

Food, drinks, parking and car wash on site mean your guests have everything in one place. You focus on the game. We handle the rest.

Company tournaments, team-building days and inter-department matches. Give your team an experience that builds bonds and breaks the office routine.

Ongoing league fixtures with regular game slots. Consistent, organised and competitive — run your league at a venue that can handle it.

Group Games
