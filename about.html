About Victory

This is Victory.

A destination built around football, designed for the complete experience.

More than a pitch.

Victory Soccer Arena was created around a simple idea: football is better when everything around it matters too. The pitch is the heart, but the experience extends in every direction.

It's where you play your game, then stay for the food. Where your children learn the sport while your car gets cleaned. Where the lights stay on all night because the game doesn't have to stop.

Victory is sport, social life, food and community — all in one place, in Kyanja, open every hour of every day.

01

Play

Quality artificial turf, floodlights and a pitch that's available whenever the game calls. Casual kickabouts, organised matches, corporate games and tournaments — all welcome.

02

Stay

Restaurant and bar on site means the experience doesn't end at the final whistle. Eat, drink, watch and relax without needing to go anywhere else.

03

Belong

Victory is a community. Friends, teams, families and regulars. A place that feels familiar from the first visit and keeps drawing you back.

The game is just the start.

From the first whistle to the last round of drinks, Victory is designed as a single, seamless experience. Play hard, eat well, stay long.
